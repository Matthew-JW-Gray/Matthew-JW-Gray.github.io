Design Experience

Selected engineering design projects from my first three years at university.

APSC 200 — P2

ATV Seat Microstructure

As part of a five-person team, we designed a porous microstructure intended to improve the comfort and safety of all-terrain vehicle (ATV) seats. The team developed and modelled a unit cell of the microstructure, tested its performance through online simulations and physical testing, and predicted the material properties and performance of a seat manufactured using the structure.

SolidWorks Design Manufacturing

MECH 323

Competative RC Car Gearbox Design

As part of a four-person team, we designed and developed a gearbox for an RC car intended to compete in both a top-speed event and a hill-climb challenge. Components were designed in SolidWorks, with gear ratios and analytical equations used to iterate and optimize the design for each event. An analytical performance analysis was then conducted to evaluate the gearbox and confirm that the final design met the requirements of both challenges.

Mechanical Design Analysis CAD

APSC 103

Formula SAE Rear Subframe redesign

As part of a six-person team, we redesigned a Formula SAE rear subframe with the goal of reducing weight while maintaining or improving structural stiffness. The redesign also incorporated a quick-disconnect system. My role involved SolidWorks modelling and FEA analysis, as well as project management.

SolidWorks FEA Design Manufacturing

APSC 101

Robotic Arm

As part of a six-person team, we designed, manufactured, and tested a assistive arm prototype. The scope of my work included analyzing, designing, and assembly of the gearbox.

SolidWorks Gear Design Manufacturing
