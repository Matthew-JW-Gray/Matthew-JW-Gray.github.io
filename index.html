Hello! I'm Matt, a Mechanical Engineering student at Queen's University. I'm in my final year here after finishing up the 16-month Queen's Undergraduate Internship Program (QUIP). Design Experience

Selected engineering design projects from my first three years at university.

APSC 200 — P2

ATV Seat Microstructure

On a team of five, we designed a porous microstructure intended to enhance the comfort and safety of all-terrain vehicle (ATV) seats. The team developed and modeled a unit cell of the microstructure, tested its performance through both online simulations and physical testing, and predicted the material properties and performance of a seat manufactured using the structure.

SolidWorks Design Manufacturing

MECH 323

Competative RC Car Gearbox Design

As part of a team of four, we designed and developed a gearbox for an RC car intended to compete in both a top-speed event and a hill-climb challenge. Components were designed in SolidWorks, with gear ratios and analytical equations used to iterate and optimize the design for both events. An analytical performance analysis was conducted to evaluate the gearbox and confirm that the final design met the requirements of both challenges.

Mechanical Design Analysis CAD

APSC 103

Formula SAE Rear Subframe redesign

Redesigned a Formula SAE rear subframe on a team of six with the goal of reducing weight while maintaining or improving structural stiffness, along with implimenting a quick disconnect system.

SolidWorks FEA Design Manufacturing

APSC 101

Robotic Arm

Designed, manufactured, and tested a small gearbox as part of a robotic arm prototype.

SolidWorks Gear Design Manufacturing
